🧑‍🎓Biography#

Guanyu Liu is Supervisor of the Hydropower and New Energy Division at the China Southern Power Grid (CSG) Power Dispatching and Control Center, and holds a Ph.D. in Atmospheric Physics and Atmospheric Environment from the Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences, School of Physics, Peking University (2026; direct admission), advised by Prof. Jing Li (NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar). From 2024 to 2025 he was a CSC-sponsored visiting doctoral researcher at Leipzig University, Germany, working with Prof. Johannes Quaas, a lead author of IPCC AR6. His research centers on interactions between natural aerosols and climate variability, the weather and climate effects of wildfires, and machine-learning retrievals for atmospheric remote sensing.

He has published 18 papers, including 10 first-author papers in Q1 SCI journals (cumulative impact factor above 100), with work appearing in Nature Communications, PNAS, Science Advances, Geophysical Research Letters and Science Bulletin. He led the Peking University Weiming Scholar Fellowship project on Carbon and Climate, and contributed to NSFC Distinguished Young Scholar and General Program grants and a National Key R&D Program project.

His background bridges atmospheric science, power system dispatching, remote-sensing AI and insurance/finance. At CSG he is responsible for power meteorology and renewable-energy power forecasting, and develops meteorological data-analysis tools and dispatching-oriented AI agents. Earlier internships at the China Three Gorges Corporation Research Institute, Microsoft Research Asia (AI for Science), GAGO Inc. and the Institute of Computing Technology (CAS) shaped a practice of bringing numerical modelling and machine learning into operational settings and translating research into energy dispatch, disaster risk reduction and climate services. He also focuses on online science communication at the power + meteorology interface, reaching an audience of over 20,000, and serves as a member of the Peking University Ph.D. Lecturers Group and organiser of the Blue Space seminar series.
